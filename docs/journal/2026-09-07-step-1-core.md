# 2026-09-07: step 1, the floor

The kernel moved under `booksmith.core`, the tree instruments under
`booksmith.tree`, and the copies went. The table of what went where is
`tools/migrate_layout.py`; it stays as the record of the move, the way the
key maps record the rename.

New: `core/errors.py`, `core/log.py`, `core/page.py`, `core/textnorm.py`,
`core/book.py`, `tree/imports.py` (the dependency rule as a function, held by
`tests/test_imports.py`, which also plants a violation and demands it be
named), `tests/test_roots.py` (every path built from `__file__` asserted
against a landmark). The command line maps `Unmeasurable` to 2, `Refusal` to
1 with one logged line, and argparse usage errors to 64, so 2 stays "could
not count".

## What the `__file__` checklist found

`config.py` and `schema.py` and `cyr.py` each moved one level deeper and
each had its `dirname` count corrected; the roots test now holds them.
`read/run.py` hashed `otsl.py` by a path built from its own file and now
hashes `otsl.__file__`. `doc/html.py` hashed a sibling `crop.py` that left
and now hashes `raster.__file__`; it wrote its module name as a literal and
now writes `__name__`. `replay.PKG` and `knobs.SRC` kept their depth and are
tested anyway.

## What the instruments said

Fast suite 324 (312 after step 0, plus 5 for the import rule and 7 for the
roots). Anchors 72 land (one literal patch became an attribute swap), attrs
216 resolve. Battery 293 of 293, after three uncaught mutations of one
class: a swap on the module that now OWNS a name (`core.page.KINDS`,
`core.textnorm.bare_math`, `core.book.journal_path`) did not reach a caller
that had bound the name at import time. The swap layer now asks
`page.KINDS` and `book.journal_path` through the module, the reading metric
re-imports only what it uses, and the test that reads the normalisation
registry asks `textnorm` for it. The rule that fell out: a name that moves
is asked of its owner, by attribute, at call time; a copy bound at import is
a name the battery cannot reach. Acceptance: nine reports and four records `same` through the whole
move, including `replay-annopage`, because the adapters did not move yet.
Ratchet 1105 in 21 files, every one declared (`textnorm.py` took 20 of
`text.py`'s 25: the title of the book whose nested blocks measured the latex
step). Knob registry 39 declared, 37 with readers, 2 debts, unchanged; the
readers now name `core/raster.py`.

## The step-0 review, folded in here

The reviewer of commit 1720300 found two defects and they are fixed in this
commit rather than a separate one, because the tree was already mid-move:
`--save` with a misspelt name was a blanket save of every report (now
refused with exit 2); a NaN on the measured side compared equal to any
number (now a change unless both sides are NaN). The records carry one hash
per input, so a rebuilt manifest beside byte-identical truth reads as "the
manifest moved" and the result diff follows as information. The four
records were regenerated: their `result` halves are byte-identical to the
step-0 ones.
